Privacy Policy

Privacy Guaranteed

Your Privacy & Security First

Learn how our Client-Side Web Tool Suite handles your files and data with 100% privacy.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

All operations performed within this Web Tool Suite (including PDF printing, merging, splitting, and image modifications) are processed locally inside your web browser. Your documents and files are **never uploaded to any external server**.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or track personal identification details, document metadata, or file content. Your data remains strictly confidential on your personal device at all times.

3. Cookies & Storage

This application does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics to profile users. Browser local storage may only be used to temporarily retain local UI preferences.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or security concerns regarding this Web Tool Suite, feel free to reach out to our team at **S&D IT Lab**.
